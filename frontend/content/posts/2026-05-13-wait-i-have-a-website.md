I haven't posted in a while. I could make excuses, some of them are even good ones, but I just never sat down at a keyboard and put thoughts to file.

Maybe time away is what I needed. Mostly so I could come back, see things I didn't like and change them. But also I think I came at this project as a silver bullet. And what I mean by that is when I tackled building this site I really felt lost in my job hunt. I wasn't standing out, I wasn't showing my homework. There's some truth there, but also I should have been doing more for myself not just what I thought would land me a job.

Now that's not to say I didn't enjoy making this site. I always enjoy making things, maybe a little too much as I look at a list of DIY projects and only a week until my parents come to visit for a while. But they're coming for their grandson, so any dread of half-finished network cupboards is more my own anxiety than anything. But ultimately that's the thing I've struggled with most, anxiety.

Between interviews, the economy, hell this post and my last post. Am I sharing too much? Should I just get any job I can? Is playing Gamble With Your Friends when stressed about money really relaxing?

But that's the thing, those can be valid concerns, but what am I doing about them? I don't want to not be myself wherever I end up working. Yes, money is getting tight, but I know I won't be happy if I settle. And sure a gambling game can stress me out, but so can anything else. But laughing with my friends has always been so good for my mental health.

Anyways my point, if I have one, is that I do want to try to make more posts. Try to find time for projects, whether that's tech related or not. And while I really want a job, I can't force it to happen, and I have to allow myself space to be me.
